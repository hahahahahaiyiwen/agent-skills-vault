# Structural Thinking

Offer lightweight methods for organizing and examining problems, ideas,
arguments, and documents. Apply a method when it improves understanding or
decisions, not as a mandatory reasoning recipe or output format.

These skills introduce established interpretive frameworks and what makes
their structures useful for human understanding. They do not prescribe how
an agent reasons. Their scope includes explaining an idea, organizing material,
and reviewing a human- or agent-authored document, not just formatting an
agent's response.

The `-analysis` names describe scope, not new theories or one canonical taxonomy.
Each skill identifies its foundation and the limits of its lightweight treatment.

## Methods

| Skill | Established foundation | What it makes visible |
|---|---|---|
| `hierarchical-analysis` | Concept mapping. | General-to-specific relationships, meaningful propositions, and cross-links. |
| `multi-dimensional-analysis` | General Morphological Analysis. | Dimensions, possible configurations, and compatibility constraints, without fixed dimension or category counts. |
| `causal-analysis` | Causal directed acyclic graphs. | Causal assumptions, mediation, common causes, and selection effects. |
| `systems-analysis` | Causal-loop diagrams from system dynamics. | Reinforcing and balancing feedback, interactions, and delays. |
| `argument-structure-analysis` | Toulmin's argument model. | Claims, grounds, warrants, backing, qualifications, and exceptions. |
| `temporal-analysis` | Statecharts using W3C SCXML concepts. | States, events, guarded transitions, hierarchy, concurrency, and scoped completion. |

Each method lives in its own directory under `skills`, with a standalone
`SKILL.md`. Load only the relevant method. Source links are optional background,
not required context. Frameworks remain distinct rather than being blended
into a new universal method.

## Boundaries

A framework describes useful relationships, not a fixed sequence of mental
steps. Methods can complement each other without becoming a required workflow.
Simple material may need none.

Clear structure is not evidence of correctness. Preserve author intent, scope,
uncertainty, and the distinction between evidence and assumptions. A gap can
remain explicit; it does not justify invented content, unsupported scores, or
extra work solely to fill a structure. No particular headings, diagram, matrix,
or reasoning transcript is required.

The earlier [communication research](RESEARCH.md) remains background on human
understanding, evidence integrity, and the costs of prescriptive scaffolding,
not proof that these methods improve agent performance.
